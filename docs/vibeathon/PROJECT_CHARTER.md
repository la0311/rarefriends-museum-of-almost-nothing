## Product thesis

Ordinary objects become worth keeping when the player can give them a small, personal moment of importance.

## Player promise

"Find almost nothing. Decide it matters. Let your Friend explain why."

## Product shape

One museum room; three plinths; four object outcome types; selected canonical Rare Friend as curator; simulated RF economy; approximately 3–8 minutes per session. No permanent progression or backend requirement. Keyboard and touch, mute, reduced motion, and 960 × 640 Vibeathon presentation unless explicitly changed later.

## Core loop

read → acquire permit if needed → expedition → settle → reveal object → keep or redeem → curate → direct Friend through tour → evaluate local brief → continue or final exhibition.

Settlement precedes curation. KEEP is no SDK mutation. Tour success never changes RF odds, settlement results or redemption values. Full runtime reload begins another session.

## Rare Friend role

Identity depth level 2: player-directed curator. The Friend's route and explicit Present actions determine presentation order. Never reduce the tour to decorative automatic animation; never invent NFT stats or traits.

## Economy principle

RF enables expeditions. SDK outcomes become owned aggregate inventory. Keeping preserves exhibition options. Redeeming removes one owned copy and returns its fixed simulated RF value. RF outcomes and tour results are separate systems. No unique specimen identity.
